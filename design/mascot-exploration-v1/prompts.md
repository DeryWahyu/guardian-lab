## 1. Robot Guardian

Berkas: `01-robot-guardian.png`

Prompt final:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: stand-alone mascot design candidate for Guardian Lab, an Indonesian cybersecurity service for checking suspicious URLs, converting QR codes to links, examining APKs, and using protective sandboxes.
Create exactly ONE original character, ONE full-body pose in ONE square image. This is a concept for user selection, not an interface mockup.
Style: refined modern 2D mascot illustration, crisp vector-like edges, rounded geometric forms, restrained cel shading, strong readable silhouette, clean consistent navy contours. Friendly, observant and competent, suitable for university students and families. Simple enough to read as a small website mascot.
Palette: deep navy #0F172A and muted teal #0F766E as the dominant character and clothing colors, soft white #F8FAFC and small light teal #99F6E4 accents. Natural skin color is allowed where appropriate. No neon.
Badge: one tiny simple emblem of two interlocking rounded ribbon links, navy and teal, with no letters.
Composition: full body, gentle three-quarter view facing the viewer, standing confidently, all limbs and accessories fully visible, centered and occupying about 80 percent of the square, comfortable clear margin. No cropping.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha background; no scenery, floor, cast shadow, backdrop, fake checkerboard or white rectangle.
No text, title, typography, watermark, UI panels, labels, extra character, duplicate pose, contact sheet, holographic effects, decorative floating icons, complex armor, weapons, superhero cape or sinister hacker styling.

Subject: a compact friendly cybersecurity robot, rounded rectangular head with a wide navy face visor displaying two expressive soft-white oval eyes and a small friendly mouth. Rounded navy torso, muted teal shoulder and boot details, visible compact articulated arms and legs. Small linked-ribbon badge on the chest.
Two main functional attributes: a compact teal shield integrated into one forearm with a subtle check-shaped inset, and one small inspection lens integrated into the opposite wrist. Pose: relaxed protective stance, shield held close, free hand raised slightly as if inviting the user to inspect something. The silhouette should feel polished and approachable, not bulky or militaristic.
```
