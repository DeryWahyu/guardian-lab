# Lima konsep logo baru Guardian Lab

Dibuat dengan imagegen bawaan untuk ditinjau pengguna. Urutan: Gate G, Prisma, Sayap Guardian, Koneksi, dan Tiga Pilar. Ikon tanpa wordmark; belum ada pilihan final dari putaran ini.

## Konsep 1

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE exceptionally simple and elegant contemporary brand symbol for Guardian Lab, a digital-safety platform for recognizing phishing, inspecting suspicious files, and opening links in isolation. This is an original icon-only identity exploration. Make it feel carefully drawn by an experienced identity designer: strong visual balance, distinctive silhouette, precise optical spacing, broad clean geometry and generous negative space. It must remain recognizable at favicon size. Square pure-white canvas, one centered symbol occupying roughly 55% of the canvas width, plenty of empty margin. Flat solid vector-like artwork only. No wordmark, no captions, no numbers, no labels, no surrounding typography. No shield, eye, padlock, brackets, circuits, fingerprint, generic checkmark badge, robot or mascot. No gradients, shading, texture, shadows, 3D, mockup, extra icon samples or brand-board layout. Use only the solid colors specified below. DESIGN 1 — GATE G. One compact bespoke capital G, drawn as a nearly circular open band with substantial even weight and a short carefully integrated inward return. A single diagonal incision at the opening creates the impression of a controlled entry gate. Use a distinctive chamfered terminal, beautifully balanced rounded geometry and generous clear inner space. One coherent silhouette, not assembled typed letters. Solid rose #e11d48 only. No L or other letter, no extra arrow, dot, ring or enclosure. The result should feel like a restrained confident technology signet.
