# Eksplorasi logo Guardian Lab, putaran baru

Tiga konsep ikon tanpa teks untuk ditinjau pengguna: Perisai Abstrak, Ruang Terisolasi, dan Deteksi. Dibuat menggunakan imagegen bawaan. Belum ada pilihan final dari putaran ini.

## Konsep 1

Use case: logo-brand. Design ONE original icon-only logo for Guardian Lab, an Indonesian digital-safety platform for phishing recognition, safe isolated browsing and APK inspection. The user wants a fresh direction that is SIMPLE and ELEGANT when used at small sizes on a website and as a favicon. Prioritize a memorable, immediately clear silhouette and generous negative space. Use only solid rose #e11d48 on a pure white background. Refined flat vector-style geometry with perfectly clean edges; use a few substantial shapes, no thin strokes. Center a single compact mark on a square canvas with generous even margins; the mark occupies about 58% of canvas width. No text, no letters, no initials, no GL monogram, no wordmark, no labels, no numbers, no multiple options within the image. No gradients, shading, textures, bevels, 3D, shadows, mockup surfaces, enclosing app tile, padlocks, circuit ornament, or tiny details. It must look designed as an identity, not a detailed illustration. CONCEPT 1 — FOLDED GUARD. A bespoke abstract shield formed from exactly two broad interlocking curved facets. The overall silhouette has restrained rounded upper shoulders and one soft tapering bottom point. A single generous diagonal white cut separates the two facets and suggests filtering harmful input while protecting the interior. Uncomplicated and balanced, with a quiet architectural feel. No extra border around the shield, no checkmark, no letters, no separate internal badge. Strong enough to remain legible in one ink at 24 pixels.
